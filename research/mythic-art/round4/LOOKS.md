# Six mythic looks — round 4, candidate A

Written before generation on 16 October 2026 and corrected after the build to match what was made. Design §6 governs each scene. These are art candidates only. All motion previews are labelled **offline approximation, not the shader**. Depth values are horizontal pixel travel at lean +1 on the 2304 × 1546 source canvas; lean −1 reverses them. Each candidate has a new generated back, ground and front. No world layer comes from an earlier round. The one shared medium is stained glass with heavy lead lines (owner confirmation, 16 October). Camera, world, light, palette, glass texture and layer recipe differ per card.

## Stage B direction, written before new generation

**Clean Break A redo.** Keep the near-overhead 70 mm camera and cold frost-white/steel-blue slot. Replace the workbench pane with a vast oblique octagonal frost-glass relic, mounted in silver came with cut-crystal perimeter facets and radial tracery. Keep its broad central face calm, so one perfectly straight rising diagonal cut dominates. A complete legendary sword, with an unmistakable guard, grip and pommel, has just left the upper-right end. The blade and relic are separate generated objects. A cold stone-glass sanctuary slab supports the relic; a near silver bevel touches only the bottom-left edge. Cold blue-white light, frost motes and restrained prismatic glints belong to the seam. No symbols, runes, warm gold, petals or road. Shallow travels remain +1 / +3 / +14 px, with a 3 px physical offset. All three world sources are new. The former A sources remain as history and hash exclusions.

**B compositions.** Banked Fire keeps its low macro view but uses a wide blunt stone anchored against a visibly shattered column base, with one sheltered coal less than a third of the stone's height at the left-centre third; the ash ridge enters from the right. Silvering keeps its elevated shoulder view but uses a broader freestanding arch left of centre, walker closer to the lower-left corner and reflection turned across the opening; the right quarter remains clear. The Unbroken Flame keeps frontal telephoto symmetry but uses a squat, broad six-lobed lantern with three low feet, a small crown and a recessed claret niche; the rose is wider than A. Unbowed keeps the worm's-eye wide view but shows the upright walker from behind at the left third, with the sword down at the right and ash crossing in the opposite diagonal. The Known Road keeps its one-point night causeway but puts the small door at x61% and a distinctly low horizon at y73%; alternating recognisable heel-and-toe footprints lie faintly on the slabs. Clean Break B keeps the legendary overhead slot but uses an elongated rectangular frost relic with clipped corners, a descending diagonal and the complete sword just beyond the lower-right exit. Each B has a newly generated complete back, support and front. Card palettes, glass treatments and distinctive depth recipes remain within their A looks.

## Look sheets

**Banked Fire — low macro, sheltered ash.** The camera sits just above the ground, close to the stone's foot, with a 90 mm macro side view. The broad weathered stone fills the right half. An ash-skinned ember mass rests in its lee on the left-centre third; one amber fissure shows through the ash skin. The back holds one shattered pillar far to the right under a darkened (× 0.62) slate sky. The ground is coarse slate-blue glass strata. A hard diagonal grey ash ribbon streams behind the stone. The front is one pale ash-stone ridge across the lower edge. A cold diffuse key comes from the upper left; the fissure is the only warm source. Glass texture: coarse slate slab glass with near-black lead. Palette: cold blue-grey; amber is the single accent. Layers (8 textures): back −6, ground +22, ash ribbon +12, stone and ember +22, hot-core +22, front +106, diagonal ash grains +55, emission mask. Lean reveals a white-hot fissure past +0.3; time moves the ash grains. At 64 px the read is one amber streak beside a blunt dark mass.

**Silvering — elevated shoulder view, silver chamber.** The camera looks down slightly from behind the walker's left shoulder, with a 55 mm three-quarter view. The tall mirror stands at about x42 %, the real walker at x27 %, and the right quarter stays open. The world is a narrow collapsed mirror chamber with silver-white walls. The ground is uneven silver-grey rubble. The front is one dark mirror jamb on the far left. A cold-white key enters from the upper right. Glass texture: thin silver came inside heavy outer lead. Palette: silver-blue and cold white; one blood-moon crimson line belongs only to the raised reflected blade. Layers (8 textures): back −20, ground +40, mirror +40, reflection +40 with up to 30 px opposite upper-body lag, walker +40, front +86, particles +62, emission mask. The reflection stays inside the mirror at 0 and ±30 px lag. At 64 px the read is a pale tall arch left of centre.

**The Unbroken Flame — frontal telephoto, six-lobed reliquary.** An eye-height 120 mm telephoto view compresses the lantern into a centred, symmetrical rose. The world is a small soot-black reliquary recess with claret glass walls, darkened (× 0.55) so the lantern leads. The ground is a dark claret altar shelf; the lantern's feet stand on its top face. The front is two small iron cusp tips at the lower corners. The one key is the lantern's white-gold core. Glass texture: warm hammered cathedral glass. Palette: amber, frost-white and blood-moon crimson, each twice, against near-black claret. Layers (14 textures): back 0, shelf +10, lantern housing +10, chamber light +10, six panes +10 with up to 34 px inward slide, flame core +10, front +48, motes +70, emission mask. Under positive lean the six panes slide inward, take a white-gold tint and thin out, and an amber-to-white chamber light fills the rose. At 64 px the read is a round six-lobed light with a white centre.

**Unbowed — worm's-eye wide, wind-cut moor road.** The camera is at about knee height with a 24 mm wide lens looking slightly up; the horizon crosses just above the knees, so the figure towers into the sky. The armoured walker stands right of centre, full length, with a level spine, torn hanging cloak and sword point down. The back is a charcoal sky cut by hard diagonal ash bands, with a thin ember horizon. The ground is a level dark moor road, lowered so that its far edge meets the ember horizon. The front is a wind-bent dead grass fan from the lower-left edge. The key is a narrow ember-orange rim from behind. Glass texture: coarse smoked glass. Palette: neutral ash grey; ember orange is the single accent. Layers (6 textures): back −12 with 2.5° lean rotation, ground and figure +58 without rotation, front +64 with the world rotation, 40 diagonal ash streaks +75, emission mask. The front travel went from the planned +112 to +64 px, so rotation stays inside the 123 px cap. At 64 px the read is one dark vertical against grey diagonals with a thin orange edge.

**The Known Road — one-point vista, night causeway.** A 35 mm view looks along a straight road to a small arched door at about x53 %, its threshold at y69 % of the window; the horizon is at about y62 %. The converging slab lines carry the frame. The world is a leaded-glass causeway over still midnight water. The back is broad ultramarine night over low distant banks. The ground is the complete receding slab road with seven pale-gold prints and their own faint glow. The front is one gold-rimmed causeway bevel across the lower edge. The key is pale-gold backlight through the door, with a soft halo round the frame. Glass texture: large fractured ultramarine panes. Palette: deep blue night; pale gold is the single accent. Layers (9 textures): back −3, ground shear from +4 at the horizon to +90 at the near edge, backlight +4, door shadow +4, door leaf +4, arch +4, front +118, particles +30, emission mask. The leaf narrows about its left hinge under positive lean, so the slit widens; the shadow shears about the threshold. Contact is measured through the ground's row transform. At 64 px the read is converging lines to a small glowing arch.

**Clean Break — near-overhead study, cold glass bench.** The camera looks almost straight down with a 70 mm lens; the pane is turned about 18°. The front is a darkened (× 0.55) glazier's straightedge across the lower right. The key is cold blue-white light coming through the cut. Glass texture: fine crystalline frost glass. Palette: frost-white and steel blue. The pane is tinted slightly (× 0.74/0.82/0.93) so that the light in the cut is the brightest thing on it. The seam light is absent at lean −1, 0.8 at lean 0 and full from +0.25; at −1 only the dark hairline of the gap stays. Only the pane is locked to the bench; the blade has lifted. At 64 px the read is one bright diagonal across a white quadrilateral.
