## Goal

Paraphrased (`AGENTS.md`, `docs/commercial-game-delivery.md`): ship Glassvow (琉璃誓言), a Godot roguelite deckbuilder, as a commercial-quality premium game that meets the commercial rubric, first on iOS.

## Who pays, for what

Paraphrased: players buy the game once at one price. Expansion-pack IAP stays open. No price is set yet.

## Channels

- App Store (iPhone and iPad): next. TestFlight builds exist; the release candidate must pass `docs/rc-bar.md`.

- Web: glassvow.eugnel.com, the game site (privacy policy, contact); it sells nothing.
- Steam, PC and Mac: backlog; `docs/rc-bar.md` does not rule them out.

1. Pass the iOS RC bar and release on the App Store.

3. Everything else.

- Glassvow accounts: the game has no accounts, chat or online features; saves stay on the device.
