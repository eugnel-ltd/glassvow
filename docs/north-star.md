# North star

Why this repo exists and how it earns. Drafted by the orchestrator from the owner's words; the owner edits at will. Quote the owner; mark a paraphrase as "Paraphrased:". See the AI-SDLC skill, `modules/north-star.md`.

This first draft takes its facts from `AGENTS.md`, `docs/rc-bar.md`, `docs/commercial-game-delivery.md`, the privacy policy and the company's repos inventory (16 Oct 2026). The owner has not yet stated a goal in his own words for this file.

## Goal

Paraphrased (`AGENTS.md`, `docs/commercial-game-delivery.md`): ship Glassvow (琉璃誓言), a Godot roguelite deckbuilder, as a commercial-quality premium game that meets the commercial rubric, first on iOS.

## Who pays, for what

Paraphrased: players buy the game once at one price. The [signed store-presence decision](https://github.com/eugnel-ltd/glassvow/issues/243#issuecomment-5335822126) sets the launch base price at US$6.99 with Apple regional prices and no introductory discount. Expansion-pack IAP stays open as a later possibility. This decision does not prove the current store price or availability.

## Channels

- App Store (iPhone and iPad): next. TestFlight builds exist; the release candidate must pass `docs/rc-bar.md`.
- Google Play: after iOS, with the Android floor devices in `docs/rc-bar.md`.
- Web: glassvow.eugnel.com, the game site (privacy policy, contact); it sells nothing.
- Steam, PC and Mac: backlog; `docs/rc-bar.md` does not rule them out.

## Priorities, in order

1. Pass the iOS RC bar and release on the App Store.
2. Keep paid-store prerequisites current. [#426](https://github.com/eugnel-ltd/glassvow/issues/426#issuecomment-5368972109) records Apple agreement, banking, tax and EU DSA verification on 21 August 2026; current sale readiness still needs readback. The Google payments profile belongs to the later Android wave.
3. Everything else.

## Non-goals

- Glassvow accounts: the game has no accounts, chat or online features; saves stay on the device.

## Measures

- Sales and proceeds per store (eugnel-hq money sync).
- Crash-free sessions (Sentry pgnetwork/glassvow) and the commercial rubric's sign-offs.

## Marketing

Players of roguelite deckbuilders on iPhone and iPad; Traditional Chinese and English. The App Store listing and glassvow.eugnel.com first.
