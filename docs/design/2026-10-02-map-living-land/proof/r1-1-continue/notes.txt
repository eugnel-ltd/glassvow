R1.1 (#660): Back to the Road from a tap as the launch rite lands
=================================================================

Device: iPad 8 (A12, Metal, lean profile), QA app io.fol2.glassvow.qa only,
installed by qa_export.sh under the shared iPad batch lock. Every launch
carried a nonce (--qa-nonce=<label>-<epoch>) that its first row echoes; rows
whose nonce does not match their launch are dropped by summary.py.txt (none
were). The iPad's identifier is looked up at run time and is in no file.

Bench: title_bench.gd.txt (copied into the QA build as tools/r11_title_bench.gd)
behind --title-probe; qa_patch.py.txt and qa_marks_patch.py.txt are the
QA-only patches (never committed to the game). The probe stores a fresh saved
run (seed 1; --qa-act=1 Act I, 2 Act II), shows the title, records the rite's
frames (every frame while the rite runs; frame 1 is the title's first frame,
which builds the rite's pipelines), then either taps the lantern at once when
the rite lands (--qa-tap-after=0, "e0"), lands the rite with a tap 500 ms
into it and taps the lantern at once (--qa-skip-at=500, "skip"), or samples
360 title frames, waits for the warm and taps ("aw", after the warm). The
Continue row's ready_ms is the tap to a drawn, interactive map.

Arms (environment variables of one QA build; see qa_patch.py.txt):
  early / cand  the build under test (its default behaviour);
  late          as PR #664: the warm waits for the rite to land (the
                same-build control for the rite's frames);
  nowarm        the title warms nothing (video memory and frame baseline);
  norush        (b1, b3) no unpacing at the tap;
  p1, p2, piclow  pictures on 1 or 2 high-priority threads, or low priority;
  h0            a paced build works its heights out on one thread;
  kf            the prefetch's setup starts in the title's first frame.

Batches (start rows carry the build label):
  b1  r11-v1  warm from the title's first process frame, kit while the
              pictures decode, unpace at the tap. Tap at 0: 1.42-1.68 s.
  b2  r11-v2  warm before the title is built, catalogue beside the kit,
              layout hand-over, 3 low-priority picture threads. 1.00-1.07 s
              and one slow-device 1.94 s (pictures stayed serial: one
              low-priority pool thread on A12).
  b3  r11-v3  pictures as a high-priority group; catalogue in 2 ms slices.
              0.69-0.87 s; unpacing at the tap gained nothing and its
              flushes dropped frames in the flare in b2, so it was dropped.
  b4  r11-v4  picture threads 1/2/3 (high priority), no unpacing at the tap.
  final        e3d96d05 (2 picture threads, paced heights on 2 threads).
              Act I rite frame 1 rose against the control; Act II did not.
  final2       9803bd95: the prefetch lets the title's first frame go by.

Files: device-rows-exp-b1..b4.jsonl (experiments), device-rows-final-
e3d96d05.jsonl and device-rows-final-9803bd95.jsonl (one row per probe line,
tagged with its launch label), summary-*.txt (summary.py.txt over them, which
reproduces them from the JSONL), plans.txt (every batch's launch plan, in
order), device_batch.sh.txt (the batch runner, under the iPad batch lock).

The e3d96d05 batch ran 42 launches back to back: its fifth round was slow in
every arm at once (main_ready to first frame 229-390 ms, the paced build about
twice as long; late 3827 ms, h0 1517 ms, cand 2466 ms), the device rather than
an arm. The 9803bd95 batch rests 10-15 s between launches.
