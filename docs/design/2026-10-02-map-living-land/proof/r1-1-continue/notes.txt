R1.1 (#660): Back to the Road from a tap as the launch rite lands
=================================================================

Device: iPad 8 (A12, Metal, lean profile), QA app io.fol2.glassvow.qa only,
installed by qa_export.sh under the shared iPad batch lock. Every launch
carried a nonce (--qa-nonce=<label>-<epoch>) that its first row echoes; rows
whose nonce does not match their launch are dropped by summary.py.txt (none
were). The iPad's identifier is looked up at run time and is in no file.

Bench: title_bench.gd.txt (copied into the QA build as tools/r11_title_bench.gd)
behind --title-probe; qa_patch.py.txt and qa_marks_patch.py.txt are the
QA-only patches (never committed to the game). The probe stores a fresh saved
run (seed 1; --qa-act=1 Act I, 2 Act II), shows the title, records the rite's
frames (every frame while the rite runs; frame 1 is the title's first frame,
which builds the rite's pipelines), then either taps the lantern at once when
the rite lands (--qa-tap-after=0, "e0"), lands the rite with a tap 500 ms
into it and taps the lantern at once (--qa-skip-at=500, "skip"), or samples
360 title frames, waits for the warm and taps ("aw", after the warm). The
Continue row's ready_ms is the tap to a drawn, interactive map.

Arms (environment variables of one QA build; see qa_patch.py.txt):
  early / cand  the build under test (its default behaviour);
  late          this build with its warm-up held until the rite lands: PR
                #664's timing on this build's code, not PR #664 itself (it
                shares the faster pictures, catalogue, layout hand-over and
                heights). It is the same-build control for the rite's frames;
                PR #664's own numbers are in ../r1-continue (cross-build);
  nowarm        the title warms nothing (video memory and frame baseline);
  norush        (b1, b3) no unpacing at the tap;
  p1, p2, piclow  pictures on 1 or 2 high-priority threads, or low priority;
  h0            a paced build works its heights out on one thread;
  kf            the prefetch's setup starts in the title's first frame.
