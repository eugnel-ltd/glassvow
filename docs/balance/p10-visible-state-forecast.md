## Method and compatibility

The historical pilot's `incoming_on` copies the hidden run RNG to place a
multi-target Smolder leap. Search `s3` has its own honest unique-recipient tick
credit, but its blow valuation also calls this pilot forecast, inheriting the
lookahead. A separate pilot version fixes that seam without rewriting `s3`.

`p10` inherits p9's expected-hand calculation, Hand-payoff scoring, foreign-way
rider suppression and preview consumption. Its incoming forecast reads current
foes, HP, Smolder and known order. Each known Smolder tick precedes the foe's
action, spends one stack, and kills through block. Remaining stacks propagate
only when exactly one living recipient exists; ambiguous leaps earn no credit.
A finale handoff stops later blows, preserving any already counted earlier.
Stagger and Silvering keep their existing blow suppression. Detached indexed
foe overrides are read-only. No hidden RNG is read, copied or advanced.

This is conservative Smolder credit, not an exact enemy-phase simulator,
an expectation over hidden branches, or a proof of the maximum damage in every
possible future. It does not add policy-weight tuning or new future information.

`p8-d0-v3` and `p9` keep the exact old forecast body and preview call sites.
`s1`, `s2` and `s3` remain selectable and their source is unchanged. Existing
Dusk invariance pins and historical readouts are not re-pinned or renamed.

The inherited Phantom Blades damage-preview omission is separate. Fixing shared
`CombatRules.preview_play` can change historical pilot target rankings. This
outcome therefore leaves that API and its callers unchanged; a later repair
must preserve old-version preview consumption explicitly and prove it.
