**Reading.**
- **Layer 1 now meets C1 and C2.** Tithes holders make attrition:fat a real lane (V0 75.0% in-cell), crown holders keep smolder:fat, and random builds fall back (arm 2 V0 44% → 32%, with knob 1).
- **CEM does not hold the fork lanes.** Every fork start (smolder:fat, attrition:fat) drifts out, mostly to shatter:fat or shatter:thin. This is the risk named in lock §8 (fork islands stay only if their fork is taken in most holdout runs; 3 of 6 boss relics are offered) and in the independent review (INFERRED).
- **V5 islands underperform.** V5 holdout ceilings sit at 17.5–38.5%, below the layer-1 fat cells (45–52%). Knob 1's harder act-1 boss may weigh more on CEM's 200-seed holdout than on the fixed-policy layer 1 (INFERRED; not probed).

The lock's remaining levers do not address C3, so the lane stops here.
