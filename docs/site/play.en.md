# How to play

Glassvow is a single-player card game: walk east from the Vigil with a lantern, build a deck of glass, kindle the lantern, and shatter what blocks the way.

## The road

A fire was cut into six pieces and scattered along the road. Gather what was broken. Light the rose. Open the sealed door at the far end.

## The cards

It is also a roguelite deckbuilder. Build a deck of glass. Kindle the lantern. Shatter what blocks the way.

Losses return you to the road. There is no account and no online play.

## On your device

Glassvow is coming soon to iOS (App Store), Android (Google Play), Amazon Appstore and Steam (PC), in English and Traditional Chinese. Saved games stay on the device. Deleting the game removes them.
