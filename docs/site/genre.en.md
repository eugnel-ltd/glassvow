# A roguelite deckbuilder

Glassvow is a single-player roguelite deckbuilder told as a stained-glass pilgrimage, coming soon to iOS (App Store), Android (Google Play), Amazon Appstore and Steam (PC).

## Games like Glassvow?

Glassvow is itself a single-player roguelite deckbuilder.

This page does not rank other titles or borrow their names. The comparison is to the genre: turn-based cards, a deck you build, a road you walk again after a loss, in English or Traditional Chinese, with no accounts and no ads.

## What that means here

Roguelite deckbuilder, in this game, means you build a deck of glass, spend light, and shatter what blocks the way. The story is a stained-glass pilgrimage: a fire cut into six pieces, a lantern, a rose to light, a sealed door at the far end.

Players who want a single-player, turn-based card game on a phone, a tablet or a PC. EUGNEL LTD makes it. How to start is on [How to play](/play/). Short answers are on the [FAQ](/faq/).
