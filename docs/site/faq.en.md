# FAQ

Glassvow (琉璃誓言) is a single-player card game for iPhone and iPad. Short answers first; detail follows.

## What is Glassvow?

Glassvow (琉璃誓言) is a single-player card game for iPhone and iPad.

It is also a stained-glass pilgrimage and a roguelite deckbuilder. EUGNEL LTD makes it. This site explains the game; it does not sell it.

## Who makes Glassvow?

EUGNEL LTD makes Glassvow.

Privacy questions go to hello@eugnel.com. Player support is glassvow@eugnel.com. Company details are on the [Company information](/company/) page.

## Does Glassvow have accounts, ads or in-app purchases?

No. Glassvow has no accounts, advertising or in-app purchases.

Saved games stay on your device. Deleting the game removes them. The game may send technical crash reports to Sentry unless you switch diagnostics off in Settings. See the [privacy policy](/privacy/).

## What kind of game is Glassvow?

Glassvow is a roguelite deckbuilder and a stained-glass pilgrimage.

You build a deck of glass, spend light, and walk east from the Vigil with a lantern. There is a longer note under [How to play](/play/) and [A roguelite deckbuilder](/genre/).

## Which languages does Glassvow use?

English and Traditional Chinese (繁體中文).
