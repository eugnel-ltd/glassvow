The signed launch base price is US$6.99 with Apple's regional prices and no introductory discount; the store's actual price must be checked before release.

Glassvow has no sign-in, account, advertising, web checkout or in-app purchase in the current product. Your saved games and settings stay on your device. The game does not upload or sync them. Your App Store purchase and licence are subject to Apple's applicable terms. These draft terms add no subscription, separate entitlement service or right to charge you again for the purchased base game.

If it is not, you may have rights to repair, replacement or a refund. Nothing here removes your statutory rights or excludes liability that cannot lawfully be excluded, including for fraud or death or personal injury caused by negligence.

## Proposed refund policy

Apple handles payment for the App Store download. To request a refund for an Apple purchase, use [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/) under Apple's applicable process. If you cannot resolve a problem through that process, contact glassvow@eugnel.com and explain what went wrong. Do not send card details, passwords or store credentials.

Your statutory rights still apply when digital content is faulty or not as described. A store's refund process does not remove those rights. Cancellation rules for digital downloads depend on the applicable law and the consent given at the store's checkout. Glassvow has no checkout of its own, so this document makes no claim that the game collects cancellation consent.

The store controls the effect of a refund on the purchased licence. This draft does not promise a game-side remote account deletion, entitlement revocation or loss of locally saved progress.
